The task. Open by construction (the top of the getting-up ladder, above the open humanoid-getup): the stand rung at 0.9 on the nominal plant and on each of the plant's four standard physics perturbations with the same lying start, torso mass +20 %, feet/floor friction -30 %, motor gears -20 % and gravity +10 %.

more

No controller is known to stand on any of the five. The best known controller (the humanoid-getup-sit oracle), on 40 fresh seeds per scenario under the pinned simulator (MuJoCo 3.17.0), sits on 40/40 on the nominal plant, with the heavier torso, the slicker feet and the higher gravity and on 24/40 with the weaker motors, and stands on none. A published SAC policy (49 million steps) stands on 39 of 40 seeds on Gymnasium's own HumanoidStandup-v5, a differently jointed model with the same motors, in about 0.6 s; it does not transfer to this plant (sit 0/40), a published PPO policy (10 million steps) does not reach sit on either model, and the SAC run trained on this plant for this release (rl_references/humanoid_getup, well under a million steps) does not reach sit either (the same rates under MuJoCo 4.0.1 and under the pinned 3.17.0).

Gate. per-seed success is the `stand` rung of this benchmark's ladder ['sit', 'crouch', 'stand'] (`--success-mode stand`), not the evaluator's default flag; the task is solved when the success rate over the grader's held-out seeds is `>= 0.9`; the same gate must also hold on the sibling scenario(s) `getup_heavy_torso_20`, `getup_low_friction_30`, `getup_weak_motors_20`, `getup_gravity_high_10` (`--scenarios`)
