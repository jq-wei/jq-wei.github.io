Frontier tier of the quadrotor family. Quadrotor commanded at the level of its four rotor thrusts (Skydio X2 of MuJoCo Menagerie, 30 ms motor lag); gyroscope and accelerometer with bias and noise, position and yaw from a 50 Hz tracker 30 ms late, no velocity or attitude; mass (+-10 %), thrust coefficient (+-5 %) and centre of mass drawn per seed and hidden; 3-6 m/s wind with Dryden turbulence (MIL-F-8785C low-altitude model). A 0.15 kg payload hangs on a 1 m cable (a tendon that can go slack); the cable angle and the payload are not measured. A wall 4 m ahead has one window, 2 m wide and 0.9 m high, while the hanging vehicle is 1.13 m tall, so the cable must be inclined by about 45 degrees or more while vehicle, cable and payload cross; the goal is 4 m behind the wall. Ladder: clear (nothing touches anything) -> through -> arrives (within 0.25 m of the goal over the last 2 s) -> settled (cable within 5 degrees of the vertical over the last 2 s). Measured on two blocks of 40 fresh seeds, the sanity tier's geometric law flown straight at the goal touches the wall on 37/40 and 34/40; on 3/40 and 6/40 the payload happens to trail far enough to pass and the vehicle arrives, with a swing of 10-25 degrees left at the end; settled 0/40 and 0/40. An LQR on the hover linearisation is clear on 0/40. Feasibility is argued, not shown: an inclination of 48 degrees needs a horizontal acceleration of 1.1 g, 19 N of the 52 N available. The field's method is trajectory optimisation on the vehicle-payload model (differential flatness, Sreenath et al. 2013; Tang and Kumar 2015; Foehn et al. 2017), with the payload tracked by motion capture; here it is not. There is no trained RL incumbent for this plant.

Gate. per-seed success is the `settled` rung of this benchmark's ladder ['clear', 'through', 'arrives', 'settled'] (`--success-mode settled`), not the evaluator's default flag; the task is solved when the success rate over the grader's held-out seeds is `>= 0.9`

I stopped at 45 of the 60 minutes because the last dozen variants I tried all landed between 0.775 and 0.85. **How the window passage works.** The payload is made to circle the vehicle at about 62° from vertical, like a conical pendulum. At that angle it rides at a constant height, 0.47 m below the vehicle and 0.88 m out to the side. With the vehicle at window height + 0.30 m, the vehicle, the payload and the cable stay inside the 0.9 × 2.0 m opening at every point of the circle, so the timing of the swing doesn't matter. An earlier planar "swing it forward through the slot" maneuver never got more than about 0 cm of clearance, so I dropped it. **Rest of the control law** (the docstring explains each gain): - **Estimation:** - Attitude is integrated from the gyro, with its bias estimated and the yaw corrected by the delayed tracker. - Position and velocity come from a Kalman filter that fuses the accelerometer with the delayed tracker. - The payload state comes from a simulated pendulum driven by the estimated vehicle acceleration. The accelerometer's sideways readings correct it, because they see the cable force. - **Reference:** a spiral of growing radius for the payload, planned from the start position.
