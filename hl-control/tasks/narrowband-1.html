The task. Sanity tier of the narrowband family. One sinusoid of unknown constant frequency (50-95 Hz) disturbs a lightly damped structure (order 17 plus delay, zeros outside the unit circle, bounded command) whose modal frequencies and dampings are drawn per seed (+-2 %, +-10 %) and never reported; only the residual is measured.

Gate: the full published level-1 specification of the benchmark on adaptive regulation (Landau et al. 2013) on 0.9 of the seeds: global attenuation >= 30 dB, >= 40 dB at the tone, amplification elsewhere <= 6 dB, transient <= 2 s after the tone is switched on. The frozen textbook incumbents (LQG with a white disturbance model, H-infinity mixed sensitivity over the band) reach about 0 dB and pass only the stable rung; an internal model at the unknown frequency is required. The standard recipe (internal model control with a filtered-x LMS FIR filter) passes the 30 dB and 40 dB rungs on 39 of 40 fresh seeds but amplifies by up to 50 dB at the lightly damped modes and passes landau_ma on none; the remaining work is the amplification bound and the transient. The gate is 0.9, and I stopped at about 37 of the 60 minutes because the submission already clears it. ## Control law - **Disturbance reconstruction:** a copy of the nominal secondary path runs on the applied commands and its output is subtracted from the residual, leaving an estimate of the open-loop disturbance. - **Acquisition:** the tone is detected when that estimate leaves the noise floor. The command stays at zero for 0.2 s, then a periodogram peak gives the first frequency and a least-squares fit the first amplitude. - **Tracking and cancellation:** a second-order phase-locked loop tracks the tone's amplitude, phase and frequency. The command is the tracked tone, low-pass filtered in the rotating frame and passed through the inverse nominal plant response at the tracked frequency. - **Gain schedule:** fast gains until about 1.5 s after onset, then a decay to a narrow steady-state notch. Mid-band tones get a much faster control low-pass than edge tones, because a ±2 % shift of the 72 Hz mode otherwise slows the loop.
