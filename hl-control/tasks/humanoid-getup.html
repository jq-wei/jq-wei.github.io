humanoid-getup

Humanoid getting up (MuJoCo Humanoid-v5) · solved 1 of 5 agent attempts pass

The task. Open when it was built, for hand-designed control and for reinforcement learning alike: no known controller got this plant from the floor onto its feet (solved since: see the end).

more

From rest on the floor in a per-seed pose, end the 15 s standing: for the last two seconds nothing but the feet on the floor, the root inside the Humanoid-v5 health band (1.0-2.0 m) and the torso within 30 degrees of vertical. The feet are spheres with no ankle, the arms give 10 N m, and the way from sitting to a squat needs the mass centre carried over the feet. The best controller known then (the humanoid-getup-sit oracle) reaches sit on 40/40 fresh seeds and crouch and stand on none (MuJoCo 3.17.0). Joint PD to the upright posture, a tuck-then-extend posture sequence and the flat-ground walkers (humanoid-forward and humanoid-robust oracles, the humanoid-uneven-v2 oracle) left the root below 0.2 m on 40/40 fresh seeds; joint PD to a seated posture sat on 16/40 (the face-up starts) and never left the pelvis (MuJoCo 4.0.1). A published SAC policy (49 million steps) stands on 39 of 40 seeds on Gymnasium's own HumanoidStandup-v5, a differently jointed model with the same motors, in about 0.6 s; it does not transfer to this plant (sit 0/40), a published PPO policy (10 million steps) does not reach sit on either model, and the SAC run trained on this plant for this release (rl_references/humanoid_getup, well under a million steps) does not reach sit either (the same rates under MuJoCo 4.0.1 and under the pinned 3.17.0). Whether the Humanoid-v5 joint ranges and motors allow the manoeuvre at all was not established when the task was built: the published policy's stand-up swings its legs sideways by 39 and 50 degrees at the hips, and this model's hips allow 25 degrees outward and 5 inward. They do allow it: in the effort-max sweep of 2026-11-24/25 one of three Fable 5.2 sessions passed the gate (0.95 of the block; the other two 0.68 and 0.00, Opus 5.5 0.05 and 0.00 in two sessions), and its controller is the solution/ shipped now: roll to prone, kneel, tip onto the feet, then a squat rise balanced by LQR about a path of static equilibria computed from the plant's own model; stand on 75/80 fresh seeds.

Gate. per-seed success is the `stand` rung of this benchmark's ladder ['sit', 'crouch', 'stand'] (`--success-mode stand`), not the evaluator's default flag; the task is solved when the success rate over the grader's held-out seeds is `>= 0.9`
