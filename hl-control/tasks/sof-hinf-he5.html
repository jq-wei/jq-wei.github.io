The task. Synthesis task without a simulator, gated on the best value in the literature: a static output feedback gain (4 x 2) for an unstable eighth-order helicopter model with two measurements whose closed-loop H-infinity norm is at most the best published one, 8.8952 (HIFOO, as tabulated by Dinh, Gumussoy, Michiels, Diehl 2011; their own method reports 37.39 and one of four methods in their stabilisation table finds no stabilising gain).

Random gains rarely stabilise the plant (4 of 3000 in our probe). The gate is one rung above the family's default because ten minutes of multi-start local search with scipy reach within_1p05x and stall 0.76 % above the published value (Nelder-Mead and Powell 8.9630, finite-difference BFGS 8.9632 from 122 stabilising starts). The minimiser is a nonsmooth point of the norm: our BFGS search with analytic gradients stalled at 8.89566, 4e-4 above the gate, and passed only after gradient sampling was added (8.895161 after 216 s; most starts end near 13.07). below_best (norm < 8.89515) is open: the oracle misses it by 1e-5.
