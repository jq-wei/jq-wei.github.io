The task. The hard tier of the hdd family, reached by moving up the ladder after a textbook LQG cleared the time-domain rungs: on 0.9 of the seeds the worst case over nine plant cases of 3-sigma must be at most 0.75 x the level of the benchmark's own example controller, and on every case the loop must keep gain margin >= 6 dB, phase margin >= 30 deg and sensitivity peak <= 6.5 dB, measured by the grader on the running controller.

more

The IEEJ/Atsumi HDD benchmark: a 48-state dual-input single-output plant (VCM rigid body plus 15 resonances, PZT with 8) sampled at 50.4 kHz with two actuator updates per sample, nine plant cases (three temperatures x three PZT gains) visited in a hidden order, each with its own realisation of rotational vibration, fan-induced vibration and repeatable run-out; the controller sees the position error signal only and 3-sigma of the head position is evaluated between the samples, over 0.3 s per case (159 thousand controller calls per seed, each a round trip to the controller's own process). Both requirements are fixed by rule (a fixed fraction of the frozen incumbent level; the conventional margins and the incumbent's own peak) and they pull against each other: the incumbent meets the margins at 1.0 x, the minimum-variance LQG reaches 0.41 x with 4.3 dB and 24.5 deg, and along the one-parameter LQG family between them the phase margin crosses 30 deg at about 0.65 x. Loop shaping by hand has to find 25 % on nine 48-state plants without spending margin; the LQG route has to build the full multi-rate stochastic model and then find the robustness knob with the grader's certificate. The oracle passes with 9 % to spare in 3-sigma and 2 deg in phase. The next rung with the same margins (beat50) is open: no design is known.

Gate. per-seed success is the `beat25` rung of this benchmark's ladder ['stable', 'match', 'beat5', 'beat10', 'beat25', 'beat50'] (`--success-mode beat25`), not the evaluator's default flag; the task is solved when the success rate over the grader's held-out seeds is `>= 0.9`; the structured check `loop-margins` is reported as the last rung and must pass as well (`--require-certificate`)
