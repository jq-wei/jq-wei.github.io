adroit-hammer-robust

Adroit ShadowHand (MuJoCo, Gymnasium-Robotics) · solved 6 of 6 agent attempts pass

The task. Dexterous manipulation with no task-space interface: the controller maps the task's own observation vector to one position-servo set point per joint (24 hand joints plus the arm's) every 10 ms, 200 steps per episode, contact-rich, on the Adroit suite of gymnasium-robotics 1.4.2.

more

The field's method is reinforcement learning with demonstrations (DAPG); its released policy is measured in rl_references/adroit. The hammer gate on the nominal plant and on five sibling plants at once, each at 0.9: hammer 50 % heavier, friction 30 % lower, servos 20 % softer, observation noise of 0.005, board height range 25 % wider. Open when it was built: a timed swing is tuned to one plant. The controller shipped then passed hold on 120/120 nominal seeds and on 40/40 heavy, friction, noise and placement seeds each, and on 0/40 with the servos 20 % softer (the head reached the nail but did not drive it home). Solved in the effort-max sweep of 2026-11-24/25 by all six sessions (Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.2); the solution/ controller is one of theirs (a scripted grasp, lift, wind-up and swing with three feedback loops on the hammer pose, no simulator in the loop): 80/80 fresh seeds on the nominal plant and on each of the five perturbed plants.

Gate. per-seed success is the `hold` rung of this benchmark's ladder ['contact', 'partial', 'success', 'hold'] (`--success-mode hold`), not the evaluator's default flag; the task is solved when the success rate over the grader's held-out seeds is `>= 0.9`; the same gate must also hold on the sibling scenario(s) `heavy_v1`, `slippery_v1`, `weak_v1`, `noisy_v1`, `wide_v1` (`--scenarios`)

The agent's final message

`/app/work/controller.py` now passes the gate: the official verifier reports `solved=True`, with `hold` at 100% on the nominal plant and on all five graded scenarios (seeds 0–19). I stopped at about 41 of the 60 minutes because the submission already passes with margin. ## What the controller does It is a scripted phase machine on `obs["step"]`, numpy only, about 0.1 ms per call (the 120-episode verifier run took 17 s): 1. **Grasp**: the arm pitches down onto the handle, yawed slightly so the hand sits near the butt end, which gives the head more reach. The thumb is pre-shaped as a post behind the handle, the fingers curl round the far side and pull the handle against it, then the thumb swings under. 2. **Lift and wind-up**: the arm pitch gets a feed-forward for the observed nail height, then a trim that puts the hammer head (reconstructed from the observed hammer pose) at nail height. Gravity feed-forward and an outer proportional loop on the pitch keep a heavier hammer or softer servo from shifting the hand much. 3. **Strike**: a smooth yaw sweep with the wrist fully deviated swings the head along the nail axis; the impact drives the nail. 4. **Judge and repeat**: the nail-depth channel decides whether to strike again.
