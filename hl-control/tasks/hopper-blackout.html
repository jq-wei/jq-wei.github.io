The task. Exploratory task (not one of the benchmark tiers reported in the paper; see docs/exploration_new_mechanisms.md): intermittent sensing.

more

With probability 0.015 per 8 ms control step every measured channel freezes at its last value for 12 steps (96 ms), roughly 15 % of the time, while the plant moves on; the controller's own clock (time, step) and last_action keep running. A memoryless feedback law acts on stale state during a blackout, and the hop cycle (0.3-0.5 s) is short enough that a 96 ms freeze straddles touchdown or lift-off, so the design has to detect staleness (a repeated observation) and bridge it with an internal phase or a state estimate. Physics nominal; gate forward (3 m) on 0.9 of the seeds. Open: the hopper-forward oracle scores 0 under blackouts.

Gate. per-seed success is the `forward` rung of this benchmark's ladder ['alive', 'forward', 'brisk', 'fast'] (`--success-mode forward`), not the evaluator's default flag; the task is solved when the success rate over the grader's held-out seeds is `>= 0.9`
