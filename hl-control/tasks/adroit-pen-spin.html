adroit-pen-spin

Adroit ShadowHand (MuJoCo, Gymnasium-Robotics) · open 0 of 4 agent attempts pass

The task. Dexterous manipulation with no task-space interface: the controller maps the task's own observation vector to one position-servo set point per joint (24 hand joints plus the arm's) every 10 ms, up to 2400 steps per episode, contact-rich, on the Adroit suite of gymnasium-robotics 1.4.2.

more

The field's method is reinforcement learning with demonstrations (DAPG); its released policy is measured in rl_references/adroit. Continuous in-hand rotation: the pen's axis has to turn by three uninterrupted revolutions about the vertical within 24 s (the package's 2 s episode for every quarter turn) while the pen's centre stays inside the package's 0.075 m ball and its axis within 45 degrees of the horizontal plane, and the pen is never dropped; the start pose of the pen is drawn per seed. The rate is the benchmark's default, 0.9 of the seeds: this motion has no incumbent (the DAPG pen policy turns the pen by 0.10 revolutions on average and reaches one revolution on none of 200 seeds). Open, with evidence that the per-seed criterion can be met in this model from the package's start pose and none yet for displaced starts: a staged derivative-free search on the simulator (one finger stroke after another from the saved state, CMA-ES per stroke) found an open-loop sequence that turns the pen by three uninterrupted revolutions in 19.8 s from the package's start pose (33 strokes; 0.95 rad/s against the 0.79 rad/s the gate needs; centre inside the ball at every step, never dropped: the hold rung), replayed through the evaluator (rl_references/adroit/pen_spin_open_loop.npz; open loop through contact, so it reproduces on the recording platform only, and with its set points rounded to four decimals it loses the pen after half a revolution); from displaced starts the same search reached 0.7 to 1.3 revolutions before a state it could not leave. The reference is closed loop: a library of 133 such strokes, the nearest ones played on a copy of the plant from the measured state at every decision and the best one taken. On 120 fresh seeds it turns the pen by 0.67 revolutions on average, passes the one-revolution rung on 0.03 and the three-revolution rung on 0.00, and drops the pen on 0.68 (a pen sliding out of a grip slowly is what its one-stroke prediction misses). What stops the search and the reference is not the rate but states (the pen tilted against the fingers, or at the edge of the ball) from which no known stroke goes on.

Gate. per-seed success is the `hold` rung of this benchmark's ladder ['contact', 'partial', 'success', 'hold'] (`--success-mode hold`), not the evaluator's default flag; the task is solved when the success rate over the grader's held-out seeds is `>= 0.9`
