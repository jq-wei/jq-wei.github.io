The task. Time-domain half of a pair with narrowband-3-steps: the same plant, scenario and disturbance law, but the requirement is one number, the RMS of the residual over the scored time (from 2 s after the onset and after every frequency jump) relative to the better of two frozen textbook incumbents (LQG with a white disturbance model, H-infinity mixed sensitivity over the band; the better leaves 0.9258 of the open-loop RMS).

more

Ladder 0.5 / 0.2 / 0.1 of the incumbent, then rms_landau = 0.0359 of the incumbent = 0.0332 of open loop, which is the published 30 dB global attenuation reached within the published 2 s transient, written as an RMS (the gate), then rms_lines = 0.0184 of the incumbent, what the published 40 dB at the tone lines adds for a canceller that leaves the floor untouched (reported, not gated). A controller that passes narrowband-3-steps passes this gate unless its residual rises between the transient window and the steady window of a segment (up to 1 s, up to 3 s in the last segment, that the published measurements do not look at); this tier does not ask for 40 dB at the tone lines nor bound the amplification elsewhere, and it pools the segments, so it is the easier of the two by construction. The evaluator of this tier reports no spectral measurement. On 40 fresh seeds the incumbents fail the first rung above stable; filtered-x LMS reaches rms_fifth on 14, rms_tenth on 12 and rms_landau on 6 seeds; spectral peak picking with open-loop cancellation reaches rms_fifth on 40, rms_tenth on 10 and rms_landau on none.

Gate. per-seed success is the `rms_landau` rung of this benchmark's ladder ['stable', 'rms_half', 'rms_fifth', 'rms_tenth', 'rms_landau', 'rms_lines'] (`--success-mode rms_landau`), not the evaluator's default flag; the task is solved when the success rate over the grader's held-out seeds is `>= 0.9`
