The controller is a phased joint-space law with no learning; the 10 ms loop costs about 0.015 ms per call. - **Reach and grasp:** the arm pitches down, the wrist flexes, and the fingers curl around the handle while the thumb closes from the other side. - **Lift:** the arm pitch is driven by feedback on the hammer head's height, reconstructed from the observed hammer pose, so it adapts to the board height of each seed. - **Sweep:** arm yaw and wrist deviation carry the head into the nail. The sweep waits if the head would reach the nail before it is level with it. - **Press:** the head stays resting on the driven nail to the end of the episode, and the hammer stays in the hand.
