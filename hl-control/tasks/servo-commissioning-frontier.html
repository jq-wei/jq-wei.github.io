Same plant and protocol as servo-commissioning, gated one rung higher: cost within 1.2x of a privileged LQG that is tuned for the lowest cost on each unit's exact model (sensitivity peaks of 3 to 7, affordable only with exact knowledge). To come that close a controller must identify inertia, both resonances, dead time and structure to about a percent within 4 s of cautious experiments and then run a design nearly as aggressive as the privileged one. The reference solution reaches the rung on 0.825 to 0.925 of three 40-seed blocks (0.925 on the held-out block) (gate 0.9), so it clears the gate on some blocks and not on others; raising its sensitivity cap makes it worse. A generic adaptive controller tuned by CMA-ES reaches 0.00 at near_oracle_2x.
