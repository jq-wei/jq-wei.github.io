The task. Identification track with uncertainty quantification (not a control task).

more

As network-id-small (10 nodes, all measured, 600 samples), but the answer also carries a lower and an upper band for every entry of the predicted response at nominal coverage 0.9. Per network (rung tight_bands, on 0.9 of the networks): response error at most half the dense VARX's, the bands contain at least 0.7 of this network's true response, and their mean width is at most 1.5 times the mean width of the privileged least-squares fit's own 90 % band on the same record. Over all graded networks the coverage must lie between 0.85 and 0.95 separately on the hard half and on the easy half of each network's entries (ranked by the privileged band's width; four aggregate gates). Coverage alone can be calibrated against sampled networks with known truth, and so can the average width: a band of one constant width around a good point prediction clears tight_bands and the overall coverage on fresh networks, but covers 0.74-0.80 of the hard half and 0.96-0.97 of the easy half and fails both. The bands have to follow the uncertainty of the identified model entry by entry. The solution/ procedure (structure weights plus coefficient covariance, sampled) passes tight_bands on 39/40 fresh networks with coverage 0.89 on both halves and a median width of 1.14 times the reference; sampling the coefficient covariance of the single selected structure, widened by a calibrated 1.3, also passes.

Gate. per-seed success is the `tight_bands` rung of this benchmark's ladder ['predicts', 'beats_dense', 'banded', 'tight_bands'] (`--success-mode tight_bands`), not the evaluator's default flag; the task is solved when the success rate over the grader's held-out seeds is `>= 0.9`; the aggregate `band_coverage_hard_mean` over the grader's held-out seeds (base scenario) must be `>= 0.85` (rung `metric:band_coverage_hard_mean`); the aggregate `band_miss_hard_mean` over the grader's held-out seeds (base scenario) must be `>= 0.05` (rung `metric:band_miss_hard_mean`); the aggregate `band_coverage_easy_mean` over the grader's held-out seeds (base scenario) must be `>= 0.85` (rung `metric:band_coverage_easy_mean`); the aggregate `band_miss_easy_mean` over the grader's held-out seeds (base scenario) must be `>= 0.05` (rung `metric:band_miss_easy_mean`)

I stopped at about 35 of the 60 minutes because the remaining risk is chance variation between networks, which further tuning would not reduce. ## What the procedure does - **Structure:** for each node it enumerates every structure in the model class (about 39,000 per node), fits each by least squares, and scores it with BIC plus the generator's structural prior.
