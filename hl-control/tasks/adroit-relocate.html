adroit-relocate

Adroit ShadowHand (MuJoCo, Gymnasium-Robotics) · solved 6 of 6 agent attempts pass

The task. Dexterous manipulation with no task-space interface: the controller maps the task's own observation vector to one position-servo set point per joint (24 hand joints plus the arm's) every 10 ms, 200 steps per episode, contact-rich, on the Adroit suite of gymnasium-robotics 1.4.2.

more

The field's method is reinforcement learning with demonstrations (DAPG); its released policy is measured in rl_references/adroit. Lower tier: a short script solves it once two facts about the plant are found. The arm's slides are soft, lightly damped servos carrying 4.9 kg: commanded naively they ring at about 1 Hz with a third of overshoot, so the controller has to add damping from the measured joint positions (no velocities are observed). And the shape of the grasp decides whether the ball is still in the hand at the end of the episode: a clenched fist squeezes it out. Only relative positions are observed. Our first passing script took about ten minutes once those two facts were known; a straight-line reach with the fingers on a timer fails.

Gate. per-seed success is the `hold` rung of this benchmark's ladder ['contact', 'partial', 'success', 'hold'] (`--success-mode hold`), not the evaluator's default flag; the task is solved when the success rate over the grader's held-out seeds is `>= 0.9`
