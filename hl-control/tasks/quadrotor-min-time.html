Frontier tier of the quadrotor family. Quadrotor commanded at the level of its four rotor thrusts (Skydio X2 of MuJoCo Menagerie, 30 ms motor lag); gyroscope and accelerometer with bias and noise, position and yaw from a 50 Hz tracker 30 ms late, no velocity or attitude; mass (+-10 %), thrust coefficient (+-5 %) and centre of mass drawn per seed and hidden; 3-6 m/s wind with Dryden turbulence (MIL-F-8785C low-altitude model). Four gates (0.3 m spheres) over 27 m, the same on every seed; the lap clock runs from leaving the start sphere to the last gate. The ladder is pegged to a time-optimal reference of 2.543 s computed offline for the nominal vehicle in still air (full rigid body with motor lag, thrust limits and drag; direct multiple shooting with CasADi/IPOPT, which the sandbox does not have; three meshes agree to 0.5 ms, six other initial guesses end 0.6-0.8 % slower, a point-mass bound is 10 % below): finish -> lap <= 1.5 x (3.815 s) -> 1.2 x (3.052 s) -> 1.05 x (2.670 s). The naive designs do not get there: the sanity tier's geometric law chasing the next gate finishes on 4/40 fresh seeds and never within 1.5 x; an LQR on the hover linearisation finishes on 0/40 (both overshoot the gates). There is no trained RL incumbent for this plant. Gate: t120 on 0.9 of the seeds, base scenario. Measured on 40 fresh seeds, the solution of quadrotor-min-time-150 meets t150 on 39/40 and t120 on 5/40 (mean lap 1.28 x the reference): it flies plans that use at most 72 % of the seed's thrust, and with less margin its feedback saturates in the wind. A seed 10 % heavier with 5 % less thrust has an optimum about 7 % above the reference, so t120 leaves it 12 %; t105 is below what such a seed can fly and is reported, not expected. Solved in the effort-max sweep of 2026-11-24/25 by Fable 5.2 in two of three sessions (0.97 and 0.95 of the block; Opus 5.5 0.40 and 0.53 in two sessions); the solution/ controller is one of them: it hovers behind the start for 3.5 s to identify the vehicle and the wind, then solves a minimum-time plan for this seed, tracks it and re-solves it on the way; t120 on 79/80 fresh seeds.

Gate. per-seed success is the `t120` rung of this benchmark's ladder ['finish', 't150', 't120', 't105'] (`--success-mode t120`), not the evaluator's default flag; the task is solved when the success rate over the grader's held-out seeds is `>= 0.9`
