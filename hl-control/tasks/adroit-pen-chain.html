adroit-pen-chain

Adroit ShadowHand (MuJoCo, Gymnasium-Robotics) · solved 2 of 5 agent attempts pass

The task. Dexterous manipulation with no task-space interface: the controller maps the task's own observation vector to one position-servo set point per joint (24 hand joints plus the arm's) every 10 ms, up to 1000 steps per episode, contact-rich, on the Adroit suite of gymnasium-robotics 1.4.2.

more

The field's method is reinforcement learning with demonstrations (DAPG); its released policy is measured in rl_references/adroit. Five targets in a row in one episode: a target is reached when the task's own success condition has held on 25 consecutive steps, the next one is then drawn, each has the package's 2 s, and the pen must never be dropped (the consecutive-successes protocol of OpenAI's in-hand work, on the package's pen). Only the first target is approached from the reset state, so the open-loop answer to adroit-pen covers one target of five: its table reaches the first on 1.00 and the second on 0.09 of 100 seeds. The gate rate is the single-target gate compounded per target: adroit-pen passes on 0.74 of the seeds (0.9 of the incumbent's hold rate on one target), so a chain of five passes on at least 0.74^5 = 0.22 of them. Open when it was built, for the incumbent too: the DAPG policy, trained on single targets from the reset state, reaches at least 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 targets on 0.805 / 0.290 / 0.130 / 0.055 / 0.045 of 200 public seeds (1.32 per seed) and drops the pen on 0.14. The reference shipped then chose among the adroit-pen scripts by lookahead on a copy of the plant from the measured state (as stored, slower, straight to the last posture, by way of the open hand, or after the last script played backwards): at least 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 targets on 1.00 / 0.36 / 0.10 / 0.02 / 0.00 of 120 fresh seeds (1.475 per seed), pen dropped on 0.09. What stopped it was the passage from one grip to the next: every stored script was searched from the open hand, and from a grip most of them play out differently; laying the pen down again by the last script backwards recovers the start pose only sometimes. Solved in the effort-max sweep of 2026-11-24/25 by Fable 5.2 in two of three sessions (0.28 and 0.65 of the block; Opus 5.5 0.00 in two sessions); the solution/ controller is the second of them (predictive sampling on a copy of the plant, planned per target): all five targets on 57/80 fresh seeds.

Gate. per-seed success is the `hold` rung of this benchmark's ladder ['contact', 'partial', 'success', 'hold'] (`--success-mode hold`), not the evaluator's default flag; the task is solved when the success rate over the grader's held-out seeds is `>= 0.22`
