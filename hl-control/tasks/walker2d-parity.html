That is above the TD3 reference of 4701 and 29.6 m. **How it works.** Each leg gets the same 3×22 gain matrix. It multiplies these inputs: - **Torso:** height, pitch, forward and vertical speed, pitch rate. - **Own leg:** joint angles and rates. - **Other leg:** joint angles and rates. - **Gait clock:** two harmonics at about 2.05 Hz, with the left leg half a cycle behind the right. - **A constant bias.** The output is clipped to [−1, 1]. The docstring explains what each group of gains does. **How the gains were found.** I wrote a CMA-ES search, a gradient-free method with no neural network, that ran on a stripped-down copy of the simulator matching the environment's resets and termination rules. Eight seeds per candidate: still about 3–4% falls. 3. Twelve seeds per candidate with an extra penalty for falling. **Robustness.** The final parameters fell on 1 of 192 fresh simulated seeds, plus the one official seed below.
