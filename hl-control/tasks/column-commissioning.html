2 x 2 process commissioning · solved 4 of 4 agent attempts pass

The task. Identify-then-control on an ill-conditioned 2 x 2 process with the structure of a high-purity distillation column: low gain, condition number (30-150), both time constants, dead time (2-10 min), the input directions and the wiring (actuator swap and polarities) are drawn per seed and never reported.

more

A 480 min commissioning window for the controller's own experiments with both compositions confined to the specification band, then a graded 600 min evaluation window. Gate: cost within twice that of a privileged LQG designed on the unit's exact model, on 0.9 of the units. The best fixed controller, given the wiring, settles on 0.88 of the units and reaches the rung on 0.15; a least-squares model from an open-loop experiment with a decoupler, tuned by CMA-ES, reaches it on 0.78.

Gate. per-seed success is the `near_oracle_2x` rung of this benchmark's ladder ['safe', 'stable', 'beats_fixed', 'near_oracle_2x', 'near_oracle_1p2x'] (`--success-mode near_oracle_2x`), not the evaluator's default flag; the task is solved when the success rate over the grader's held-out seeds is `>= 0.9`
