The agent's final message

`/app/work/controller.py` now passes the `brisk` rung on 100 of 100 fresh seeds (200–299) on the nominal plant. That is above the 0.9 gate. Mean speed is about 0.91 m/s, and it doesn't reach `fast`, which needs 1 m/s. I only ran the nominal `standard_v0` scenario. I haven't run the perturbation scenarios, so I can't say how it holds up on `push_v1` or `weak_motors_20`. The inherited walker stayed upright on every seed but averaged only 0.31 m/s, about 4.7 m in 15 s, so it failed `brisk`. I kept its structure and changed only the parameters. The tuner accepts at most 12, so I split `PARAMS` into a tuned set of 12 and a `FIXED` dict for the rest, and I added a line to the docstring saying so. - **First tune:** on 6 seeds it reached `brisk` on about 92% of seeds, with falls at roughly step 250. In the one case I traced, yaw drifted, the gait sped up into a run and the robot pitched forward. - **Second tune:** I swapped in a different 12 parameters, including the torso, yaw, roll and lateral-placement gains, and tuned on 40 seeds. It got 100% on a separate block of seeds 100–139 and then on the 100-seed check above. `v2.py` (the 92% version) and `v3.py` (a copy of the current controller) are scratch copies in `/app/work`, and `roll.py` is a debug rollout script. About 21 minutes of the 60 were used.
