narrowband-3-steps

Active vibration control · solved 6 of 6 agent attempts pass

The task. Hard tier of the narrowband family, the frequency-domain half of a pair with narrowband-3-rms (same plant and disturbances, requirement stated as an RMS).

more

One to three sinusoids (the number is hidden) of unknown frequency in 50-95 Hz disturb a lightly damped structure (order 17 plus delay, zeros outside the unit circle one of them inside the band, bounded command) whose modal frequencies and dampings are drawn per seed (+-2 %, +-10 %); every tone jumps by 2-5 Hz at two hidden times; only the residual is measured. Gate: the full published level-3 specification of the benchmark on adaptive regulation (Landau et al. 2013) in every segment on 0.9 of the seeds: global attenuation >= 30 dB, >= 40 dB at every tone, amplification elsewhere <= 9 dB, and the transient over within 2 s of the onset and of every jump (energy ratio <= 1.21). The frozen textbook incumbents (LQG with a white disturbance model, H-infinity mixed sensitivity over the band, both required to be stable for the +-2 % plants) reach -0.1 and +0.4 dB and pass only the stable rung. First-attempt adaptive designs fail far below the gate (40 fresh seeds): internal model control with a filtered-x LMS FIR filter reaches 30 dB on 7 seeds (single tones) and amplifies by up to 73 dB at the lightly damped modes; spectral peak picking with open-loop cancellation through the nominal model reaches 17 dB on average (20 dB on 5 seeds) with up to 50 dB of amplification; neither passes landau_ma on any seed. The reference solution needs frequency lock (for the 40 dB), a command path that does not excite the uncertain modes at 45 and 109 Hz (for stability and the 9 dB) and a slow command behind fast trackers (its neighbouring gain settings lose stability on some plants); it clears the gate with the transient ratio as the tightest rung.

Gate. per-seed success is the `landau_tr` rung of this benchmark's ladder ['stable', 'attenuate', 'landau_ga', 'landau_da', 'landau_ma', 'landau_tr'] (`--success-mode landau_tr`), not the evaluator's default flag; the task is solved when the success rate over the grader's held-out seeds is `>= 0.9`

How it works: 1. **Identification.** The tones cannot start before 1 s, so the controller spends the first 0.75 s applying a ±0.04 V pseudo-random probe and fits the eight modal frequencies and dampings of that seed's structure. 2. **Disturbance observer.** A copy of the identified secondary path runs alongside the plant, so the disturbance at the sensor is recovered independently of the command. 3. **Tone fit.** Up to three sinusoids are fitted by Gauss-Newton least squares on a sliding window. A jump in prediction error marks the onset or a frequency step and restarts the window; the first new estimate is in use about 0.19 s after a change. 4. **Command.** Each fitted tone is cancelled through the identified plant's gain and phase at that frequency.
