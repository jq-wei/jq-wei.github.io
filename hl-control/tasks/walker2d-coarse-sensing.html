The task. Exploratory task (not one of the benchmark tiers reported in the paper; see docs/exploration_new_mechanisms.md): coarse, velocity-free sensing.

more

Measured angles and heights are quantised to 0.05 rad (about 3 degrees) and no velocity channel is measured: every velocity field and the velocity half of gym_obs read zero, while the plant itself is nominal. Any gait law that uses joint rates or the torso velocity must first reconstruct them from quantised positions sampled every 8 ms, where naive differencing yields steps of 6 rad/s, i.e. an observer or filter has to come before the walking law. Gate forward (3 m) on 0.9 of the seeds. Open: the walker2d-forward oracle scores 0 on coarse sensing.

Gate. per-seed success is the `forward` rung of this benchmark's ladder ['alive', 'forward', 'brisk', 'fast'] (`--success-mode forward`), not the evaluator's default flag; the task is solved when the success rate over the grader's held-out seeds is `>= 0.9`
