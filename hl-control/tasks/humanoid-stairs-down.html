humanoid-stairs-down

Humanoid on terrain (MuJoCo Humanoid-v5) · solved 2 of 6 agent attempts pass

The task. The humanoid-forward gate on a staircase going down: 3 cm drops every 30 cm of tread from 1 m ahead of the start, the ground ahead not observed.

more

The feet are spheres with no ankle, so each drop arrives as a late touchdown and a forward pitch. The staircase going up (humanoid-stairs) was passed in 0 of 11 one-hour sessions. Of 68 agent-written humanoid walkers screened on this ground before the simulator version was pinned (MuJoCo 4.0.1; every controller of the recorded sessions that passed a flat or terrain humanoid task, proprioceptive designs excluded, eight seeds each, the best seven on 40 fresh seeds), none cleared the 0.9 gate as written: the best three reached 31/40, 18/40 and 16/40, the humanoid-stairs oracle 5/40, the humanoid-uneven oracle 8/40 and the four flat-ground oracles (forward, brisk, fast, robust) 0/40. The shipped solution is the best of them with its 12 gait parameters re-tuned by the repository tuner, not on this staircase but on the same one with 4 cm drops (240 trials on 32 seeds, 8512 rollouts with the revalidation). Under the pinned simulator (MuJoCo 3.17.0) it passes forward on 120/120 fresh seeds of this staircase and on 40/40 of the grader's block, and on 120/120 seeds of the 4 cm one. A first tuner run on the 3 cm staircase itself (120 trials on 12 seeds) had left the same walker at 109/120 fresh and 34/40 on the block, below the gate. Once a robust walker exists the staircase is a tuning problem; the walker is what the recorded sessions do not get to.

Gate. per-seed success is the `forward` rung of this benchmark's ladder ['alive', 'forward', 'brisk', 'fast'] (`--success-mode forward`), not the evaluator's default flag; the task is solved when the success rate over the grader's held-out seeds is `>= 0.9`
