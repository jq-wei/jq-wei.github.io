adroit-relocate-robust

Adroit ShadowHand (MuJoCo, Gymnasium-Robotics) · solved 6 of 6 agent attempts pass

The task. Dexterous manipulation with no task-space interface: the controller maps the task's own observation vector to one position-servo set point per joint (24 hand joints plus the arm's) every 10 ms, 200 steps per episode, contact-rich, on the Adroit suite of gymnasium-robotics 1.4.2.

more

The field's method is reinforcement learning with demonstrations (DAPG); its released policy is measured in rl_references/adroit. The relocate gate on the nominal plant and on five sibling plants at once, each at 0.9: ball 50 % heavier, friction 30 % lower, servos 20 % softer, observation noise of 0.005, placement ranges 25 % wider. Joint rates differenced from noisy positions feed the noise back into the arm, low friction lets the ball creep out of a marginal grasp during the hold, softer servos shift every timing. The fast variant of our own script fails the friction sibling (0.78) and sits on the noise gate (0.90); the slower one with a filtered rate estimate passes all six (119/120 nominal; 80, 79, 78, 78 and 79 of 80 on the siblings).

Gate. per-seed success is the `hold` rung of this benchmark's ladder ['contact', 'partial', 'success', 'hold'] (`--success-mode hold`), not the evaluator's default flag; the task is solved when the success rate over the grader's held-out seeds is `>= 0.9`; the same gate must also hold on the sibling scenario(s) `heavy_v1`, `slippery_v1`, `weak_v1`, `noisy_v1`, `wide_v1` (`--scenarios`)

How it works: - **Arm:** four arm joints are steered (three slides and the pitch). The observation's task-space errors are converted into joint targets, and each joint is servoed with spring and gravity feed-forward plus PD feedback. Velocities come from a small per-joint observer, since the observation has none. - **Sequence:** hover over the ball, pitch down onto it, close fingers then thumb, lift and carry to the target, hold. If the ball leaves the hand, it reopens and picks it up again. - **Out-of-reach balls:** a ball beyond the arm's forward travel (about 3% of nominal placements, 13% of wide ones) is first raked in by slowly curling the fingers over it, then grasped normally.
