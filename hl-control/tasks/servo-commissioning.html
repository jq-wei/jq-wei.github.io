The task. Identify-then-control on a compliant servo axis whose load inertia (x16), first resonance (12-40 Hz), structure (one or two compliant stages), dead time (1-6 steps) and actuator polarity are drawn per seed and never reported.

more

A 4 s commissioning window for the controller's own experiments under tight travel and shaft-torque limits, then a graded 5 s evaluation window. Gate: cost within twice that of a privileged LQG designed on the unit's exact model, on 0.9 of the units. The best fixed controller (given the polarity) is 4 to 15 times the privileged cost on every unit, and a generic adaptive controller tuned by CMA-ES reaches the rung on none, so no design without a real identification reaches the gate.

Gate. per-seed success is the `near_oracle_2x` rung of this benchmark's ladder ['safe', 'stable', 'beats_fixed', 'near_oracle_2x', 'near_oracle_1p2x'] (`--success-mode near_oracle_2x`), not the evaluator's default flag; the task is solved when the success rate over the grader's held-out seeds is `>= 0.9`
