adroit-door-robust

Adroit ShadowHand (MuJoCo, Gymnasium-Robotics) · solved 6 of 6 agent attempts pass

The task. Dexterous manipulation with no task-space interface: the controller maps the task's own observation vector to one position-servo set point per joint (24 hand joints plus the arm's) every 10 ms, 200 steps per episode, contact-rich, on the Adroit suite of gymnasium-robotics 1.4.2.

more

The field's method is reinforcement learning with demonstrations (DAPG); its released policy is measured in rl_references/adroit. The door gate on the nominal plant and on five sibling plants at once, each at 0.9: door and latch 50 % heavier, friction 30 % lower, servos 20 % softer, observation noise of 0.005, door placement ranges 25 % wider. Open when it was built: the controller shipped then passed hold on 79/80 nominal seeds and on 40/40 heavy, 33/40 friction, 31/40 servos, 31/40 noise and 34/40 placement seeds. Solved in the effort-max sweep of 2026-11-24/25 by all six sessions (Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.2); the solution/ controller is one of theirs (resolved-rate inverse kinematics on a copy of the plant's model, kinematics only, with a task-space integrator): 1.00 nominal and 1.00 / 1.00 / 1.00 / 0.99 / 0.93 on the five perturbed plants, 80 fresh seeds each.
