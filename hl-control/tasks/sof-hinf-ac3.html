The agent's final message

The task passes: `/app/work/controller.py` gives a closed-loop H∞ norm of **γ = 3.402304**, which beats the published best of 3.4859 by about 2.4%. The evaluator passes all five rungs on seeds 0–9, including `below_best` (threshold γ < 3.48585), so the gate passes with a margin of about 0.08. It took about 8.5 of the 60 minutes. **The control law** is `u = K y`, with K a fixed 2×4 matrix typed into the file at full precision. Its largest entry is 1835, well under the grader's 1e4 limit. The closed loop is stable (spectral abscissa −0.411), and the norm peaks equally at two frequencies, ω = 0 and ω ≈ 1.47 rad/s. **How I found K:** - **First attempt:** a multi-start BFGS search, the same kind of method HIFOO uses, minimising the norm with no limit on K. Every run drifted to gains of 2e4 up to 1e8 and stalled near 3.64 or 4.53. All of those gains break the 1e4 limit. At such gains the loop passes the disturbance almost directly into u, which keeps the norm from dropping below about 3.64. - **What worked:** keeping every entry of K below the limit and adding a penalty on the size of K that shrinks to zero over the run. That moved the search into a different basin: 22 of 25 starts finished between 3.41 and 3.48 with max |K| around 1–2e3. - **Polishing:** restarting the search from the best points brought them to 3.402–3.404.
