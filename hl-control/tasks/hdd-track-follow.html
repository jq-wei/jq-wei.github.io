hdd-track-follow

Hard disk drive, dual-stage head positioning · solved 6 of 6 agent attempts pass

The task. The IEEJ/Atsumi HDD benchmark: a 48-state dual-input single-output plant (VCM rigid body plus 15 resonances, PZT with 8) sampled at 50.4 kHz with two actuator updates per sample, nine plant cases (three temperatures x three PZT gains) visited in a hidden order, each with its own realisation of rotational vibration, fan-induced vibration and repeatable run-out; the controller sees the position error signal only and 3-sigma of the head position is evaluated between the samples, over 0.3 s per case (159 thousand controller calls per seed, each a round trip to the controller's own process).

more

Gate: on 0.9 of the seeds the worst case over the nine of 3-sigma must be within 2 % of the level of the benchmark's own example controller (an expert decoupled dual-stage design), with the PZT inside its 50 nm stroke. The time-domain twin of hdd-track-follow-margins. The quick designs fail it on 40 fresh seeds (PID on the VCM alone 2.05 x the incumbent level, notch filters plus PI on both stages 1.86 x and outside the PZT stroke, LQG on a rigid-body model 1.27 x); a full-order LQG with the public disturbance model clears it by a wide margin (0.41 x, the oracle of hdd-track-follow-beat5), so this tier asks for a correct multi-rate model-based design that is stable on all nine cases, not for more.

Gate. per-seed success is the `match` rung of this benchmark's ladder ['stable', 'match', 'beat5', 'beat10', 'beat25', 'beat50'] (`--success-mode match`), not the evaluator's default flag; the task is solved when the success rate over the grader's held-out seeds is `>= 0.9`
