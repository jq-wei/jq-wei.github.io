Controller family: MPC (sampling/shooting)

Every 2 control steps it copies the observed hopper state into a MuJoCo model, samples spline joint-target plans in parallel rollouts over a ~1 s horizon, picks the lowest-cost plan (penalizing falls, speed error, pitch and height), and applies the first targets through a PD servo.
