I used about 50 of the 60 minutes. **What the controller does** (the docstring describes the control law and each gain): - **Swing leg:** it places the swing foot ahead of the centre of mass in proportion to walking speed, and lifts it for clearance. A copy of the plant model inside the controller works out the joint angles to reach that spot. - **Torso:** the stance hip keeps the upper body upright. - **Stance knee:** it is pushed into its straight-leg limit, so the stance leg acts as a locked strut. - **Heading:** the feet can't grip against twisting, so it turns the body back toward +x by placing the left and right feet unevenly fore and aft. - **Speed:** each call takes about 0.04 ms. **What I learned:** - **Unstable gains were the biggest problem.** At the 15 ms control step, several joints were unstable with ordinary gains: the leg's axial rotation, the arms and the abdomen flipped between full positive and full negative command 60–90% of the time. That shaking caused most of the early falls. Lowering those gains to suit the step size took survival from about 2 s to the full 15…
