On seeds the tuning never saw (parameters were tuned on seeds 0–47 only), it holds up: | Seeds | Mean return | Hold rate | | --- | --- | --- | | 900–1399 | 3049 | 0.996 | | 1400–2399 | 3042 | 0.993 | | 2400–2899 | 3028 | 0.986 | ## How it works It is a four-phase state machine driven by the measured handle point, latch angle and door angle: 1. **Reach:** the palm goes to a hover point above the lever with the fingers pre-curled into a hook. 2. **Turn:** the palm target is rotated about the latch axis ahead of the measured latch angle while the forearm rolls with it, so the palm pushes the lever down along its arc and the fingers close behind it. 3. **Pull:** once the latch is past about 1.3 rad the palm target leads the handle, first straight out of the door plane, then along the handle's swing circle. The fingers start opening at 0.71 rad. 4. **Release:** at about 0.83 rad the arm retreats straight back out of the door's swing circle and the door coasts to its stop. The arm runs a task-space PD on the measured palm point through the inverse arm Jacobian, with the servo spring cancelled so the command is a pure force.
