adroit-pen-chain-2

Adroit ShadowHand (MuJoCo, Gymnasium-Robotics) · solved 6 of 6 agent attempts pass

The task. Dexterous manipulation with no task-space interface: the controller maps the task's own observation vector to one position-servo set point per joint (24 hand joints plus the arm's) every 10 ms, up to 400 steps per episode, contact-rich, on the Adroit suite of gymnasium-robotics 1.4.2.

more

The field's method is reinforcement learning with demonstrations (DAPG); its released policy is measured in rl_references/adroit. Two targets in a row in one episode, the lower tier of adroit-pen-chain: a target is reached when the task's own success condition has held on 25 consecutive steps, the second one is then drawn, each has the package's 2 s, and the pen must never be dropped. The first target is approached from the reset state, the second from wherever the first ended, which is the one step the open-loop answer to adroit-pen does not have (its table reaches a second target on 0.09 of 100 seeds, measured in the five-target chain). The gate rate is the single-target gate compounded per target: adroit-pen passes on 0.74 of the seeds (0.9 of the incumbent's hold rate on one target), so a chain of two passes on at least 0.74^2 = 0.55 of them. Open when it was built, for the incumbent too: the DAPG policy, trained on single targets from the reset state, reaches the first target on 0.805 and both on 0.290 of 200 public seeds (1.095 per seed) and drops the pen on 0.100. The reference shipped then, a lookahead controller (the adroit-pen scripts chosen on a copy of the plant from the measured state at every new target), reached both targets on 43/120 fresh seeds (1.358 per seed), pen dropped on 0.06. What stopped it was the passage from the first grip to the second target: every stored script was searched from the open hand, and from a grip most of them play out differently. Solved in the effort-max sweep of 2026-11-24/25 by all six sessions (Opus 5.5 at 0.57-0.82 of the block, Fable 5.2 at 0.62-0.97); the solution/ controller is one of theirs (pose sequencing: a hub pose, a table of poses for the first target, a best-first search on a copy of the plant for the second): both targets on 77/80 fresh seeds.

Gate. per-seed success is the `hold` rung of this benchmark's ladder ['contact', 'partial', 'success', 'hold'] (`--success-mode hold`), not the evaluator's default flag; the task is solved when the success rate over the grader's held-out seeds is `>= 0.55`

The first target was reached on every one of these seeds; the three failures are second targets that ran out of time. ## How it works - **Exact copy of the plant.** The controller steps its own copy of the package's model with the actions it has returned. The reset state is fixed and the simulation is deterministic, so the copy matches the plant at every step (checked: no mismatch in any run). - **Hub pose.** From reset the hand holds one fixed pose for 60 steps, which props the pen up on the fingers. - **First target by table.** A library of poses, found offline by a derivative-free search on the simulator (no networks, no gradients), is rolled out once from the hub state at start-up. The controller picks a pose whose trajectory holds the target, preferring ones that leave the pen easy to move afterwards. - **Second target by search.** From the true state it tries candidate poses in the copy, commits the best as the next phase, and repeats until one holds the target for 25 s…
