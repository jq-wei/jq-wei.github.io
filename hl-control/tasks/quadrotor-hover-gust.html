quadrotor-hover-gust

Quadrotor in gusts (Skydio X2, MuJoCo) · solved 6 of 6 agent attempts pass

The task. Sanity tier of the quadrotor family: textbook material, expected to be solved in every session.

more

Quadrotor commanded at the level of its four rotor thrusts (Skydio X2 of MuJoCo Menagerie, 30 ms motor lag); gyroscope and accelerometer with bias and noise, position and yaw from a 50 Hz tracker 30 ms late, no velocity or attitude; mass (+-10 %), thrust coefficient (+-5 %) and centre of mass drawn per seed and hidden; 3-6 m/s wind with Dryden turbulence (MIL-F-8785C low-altitude model). Gate: error from the setpoint <= 0.25 m after 4 s on 0.9 of the seeds, on the base scenario and on four perturbations (mass +-20 %, thrust -15 %, motor lag 60 ms, wind 8-11 m/s). Measured on 40 fresh seeds: the solution holds 40/40 on the base scenario and 40/40, 40/40, 40/40, 39/40 on the perturbations (and reaches the tight rung, RMS <= 0.05 m, on 40/40); an LQR on the hover linearisation without integral action holds 38/40 base seeds; the zero-thrust starter 0/40. There is no trained RL incumbent for this plant.

Gate. per-seed success is the `hold` rung of this benchmark's ladder ['airborne', 'coarse', 'hold', 'tight'] (`--success-mode hold`), not the evaluator's default flag; the task is solved when the success rate over the grader's held-out seeds is `>= 0.9`; the same gate must also hold on the sibling scenario(s) `mass_v1`, `weak_thrust_v1`, `slow_motor_v1`, `windy_v1` (`--scenarios`)
