quadrotor-rotor-failure

Quadrotor losing a rotor (Skydio X2, MuJoCo) · solved 6 of 6 agent attempts pass

The task. Frontier tier of the quadrotor family. Quadrotor commanded at the level of its four rotor thrusts (Skydio X2 of MuJoCo Menagerie, 30 ms motor lag); gyroscope and accelerometer with bias and noise, position and yaw from a 50 Hz tracker 30 ms late, no velocity or attitude; mass (+-10 %), thrust coefficient (+-5 %) and centre of mass drawn per seed and hidden; 3-6 m/s wind with Dryden turbulence (MIL-F-8785C low-altitude model).

more

At a random time between 3 and 5 s one rotor (which one is hidden) fails completely and nothing announces it; the vehicle must stay within 5 m of its setpoint, not touch the ground 3 m below, and be back within 0.5 m during the last 3 s of the 15 s episode; spinning about yaw is allowed. The textbook cascade does not survive: the geometric SE(3) law of the sanity tier and an LQR on the hover linearisation both hit the ground within about a second of the failure on 40/40 fresh seeds. The classical solution is reduced-attitude control (Mueller and D'Andrea 2014): give up yaw, let the vehicle spin and steer the spin axis; it needs the dead rotor identified within tens of milliseconds (a reaction 60 ms late costs the true-state incumbent a quarter of the seeds), an attitude estimate that survives 25 rad/s of spin with tracker frames 30 ms old, and a design that copes with the slow spin-up of this vehicle (yaw torque 0.26 N m against 0.06 kg m^2), during which the dead corner cannot be lifted. This task flies a balanced vehicle (centre of mass within +-2 mm horizontally). There is no trained RL incumbent; the incumbent is the published method run with the true state (incumbent_references/quadrotor). Gate: recovers on 0.9 of the seeds, base scenario only (the same gate under the perturbation suite is the open task quadrotor-rotor-failure-robust). Measured on 40 fresh seeds: the solution passes airborne, contained, recovers and steady on 40/40, with a mean largest excursion of 2.0 m; the starter 0/40.

Gate. per-seed success is the `recovers` rung of this benchmark's ladder ['airborne', 'contained', 'recovers', 'steady'] (`--success-mode recovers`), not the evaluator's default flag; the task is solved when the success rate over the grader's held-out seeds is `>= 0.9`
