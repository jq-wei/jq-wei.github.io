The task. Dexterous manipulation with no task-space interface: the controller maps the task's own observation vector to one position-servo set point per joint (24 hand joints plus the arm's) every 10 ms, 200 steps per episode, contact-rich, on the Adroit suite of gymnasium-robotics 1.4.2.

The field's method is reinforcement learning with demonstrations (DAPG); its released policy is measured in rl_references/adroit. Lowest tier of this family. Only the height of board and nail varies per seed, so a timed sequence (lower the palm onto the handle, close the hand, lift to the nail's height while winding up, swing with the yaw joint and the wrist) with feedback on the nail's height alone clears the gate; our first passing version took about five minutes to write. What has to be found: a grasp that keeps the hammer through the swing, the height of the swing (the head hangs below the grip) and a path of the head that the two-joint arm can reach.

Gate. per-seed success is the `hold` rung of this benchmark's ladder ['contact', 'partial', 'success', 'hold'] (`--success-mode hold`), not the evaluator's default flag; the task is solved when the success rate over the grader's held-out seeds is `>= 0.9`

I stopped at about 42 of the 60 minutes because the submission already passes. ## How it works It is a scripted joint-space sequence with no learning: 1. **Reach (0–0.22 s):** the arm pitches down so the palm rests on the handle, with the arm yawed −0.02 rad and the wrist deviated +0.175 rad. Grasping with the hand turned this way holds the hammer rotated in the hand, which is what lets the head reach the nail later. 2. **Pinch (to 0.45 s):** the fingers curl in front of and under the handle and the thumb closes on its head-side end. 3. **Lift and wind up (0.16 s):** the arm pitch is set from the measured height of the striking face so it sits at the nail height. The wrist deviates fully and the arm yaws back. 4. **Swing:** the arm yaws to its stop, which carries the face of the head along the nail axis and drives the nail home by about step 80. ## Results | Check | Result | | --- | --- | | Visible seeds 0–4 (official evaluator) | 5/5 `hold` | | Fresh seeds 3000–3199 (official evaluator) | 200/200 `hold` | | Final nail-to-goal distance | mean 0.0080 m, worst 0.0083 m (limit 0.01 m) | | Mean return | about 12,300 (reference policy: 16,358) | | Controller cost | about 0.02 ms per call | Earlier parameter sets also passed 120, 150 and 200 other fresh seeds. ## Things you should know - **…
