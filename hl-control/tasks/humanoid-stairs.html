Built after the paper's sweep to test whether a task can force a mechanism outside the catalogue (docs/exploration_new_mechanisms.md, docs/soft_arm_spec.md). The -proprio tasks replace the full-state observation by on-board sensing: encoders, IMU and foot contacts on the humanoid, pressures and bend angles on the soft arm. Not part of the benchmark tiers or of the paper's main counts; the soft arm is reported in the paper as a plant outside the sweep, the other tasks are not; all sessions ran under the paper's protocol.

The task. Exploratory task (not one of the benchmark tiers reported in the paper; see docs/exploration_new_mechanisms.md): the humanoid-forward gate on a staircase going up, 3 cm risers every 30 cm of tread from 1 m ahead of the start, the ground ahead not observed.

more

The feet are spheres with no ankle, so a riser is cleared by lifting the swing foot, not by rolling over it. Every repository gait and 42 of the 44 passing agent-written humanoid walkers of the flat tasks (robust and parity tasks included) fall at the first riser: their swing foot clears the ground by 1-2 cm and catches the step; the two that pass lift the swing foot 5-8 cm and step on a clock. The same staircase with 5 cm risers, or going down, has no known solution.

Gate. per-seed success is the `forward` rung of this benchmark's ladder ['alive', 'forward', 'brisk', 'fast'] (`--success-mode forward`), not the evaluator's default flag; the task is solved when the success rate over the grader's held-out seeds is `>= 0.9`

It is still the best version I found, and it is what's saved in `/app/work/controller.py`. **How it works (written up in the file's docstring):** - **Point feet:** the feet are spheres, so the robot can't stand still; a pure joint-hold posture falls within about 1 s. It has to keep stepping. - **Gait timing:** a short settle phase, then a stance/swing state machine. The legs swap when the swing foot touches down after half a step period, or at the latest after 1.6 periods. - **Torso:** the stance hip holds torso pitch and roll steady and cancels the swing hip's reaction torque. - **Foot placement:** each swing foot target is set from the centre-of-mass velocity in a frame aligned with the world, so the robot heads along +x even when its body turns. A slow integral term adjusts the target until walking speed settles at the desired value. - **Clearing the risers:** the swing knee bends, with extra hip flexion, early in the step to lift the foot over the 3 cm risers. **What made the difference:** - **Low derivative gains:** high values made the commands flip between ±0.4 every step; about 3–5 N m s/rad works (the joints already have damp…
