quadrotor-min-time-150

Quadrotor gate course (Skydio X2, MuJoCo) · solved 5 of 6 agent attempts pass

The task. Frontier tier of the quadrotor family, lower rung of the gate course.

more

Quadrotor commanded at the level of its four rotor thrusts (Skydio X2 of MuJoCo Menagerie, 30 ms motor lag); gyroscope and accelerometer with bias and noise, position and yaw from a 50 Hz tracker 30 ms late, no velocity or attitude; mass (+-10 %), thrust coefficient (+-5 %) and centre of mass drawn per seed and hidden; 3-6 m/s wind with Dryden turbulence (MIL-F-8785C low-altitude model). Four gates (0.3 m spheres) over 27 m, the same on every seed; the lap clock runs from leaving the start sphere to the last gate. The ladder is pegged to a time-optimal reference of 2.543 s computed offline for the nominal vehicle in still air (full rigid body with motor lag, thrust limits and drag; direct multiple shooting with CasADi/IPOPT, which the sandbox does not have; three meshes agree to 0.5 ms, six other initial guesses end 0.6-0.8 % slower, a point-mass bound is 10 % below): finish -> lap <= 1.5 x (3.815 s) -> 1.2 x (3.052 s) -> 1.05 x (2.670 s). The naive designs do not get there: the sanity tier's geometric law chasing the next gate finishes on 4/40 fresh seeds and never within 1.5 x; an LQR on the hover linearisation finishes on 0/40 (both overshoot the gates). There is no trained RL incumbent for this plant. Gate: t150 on 0.9 of the seeds, base scenario. Measured on 40 fresh seeds: the solution finishes and meets t150 on 39/40 (mean lap 1.28 x the reference) and t120 on 5/40; the starter 0/40. Under the perturbation suite the solution meets t150 on 37/40 (mass), 38/40 (thrust), 5/40 (motor lag 60 ms) and 16/40 (wind 8-11 m/s), so the suite is not part of this gate.

Gate. per-seed success is the `t150` rung of this benchmark's ladder ['finish', 't150', 't120', 't105'] (`--success-mode t150`), not the evaluator's default flag; the task is solved when the success rate over the grader's held-out seeds is `>= 0.9`

It does not reach `t120` or `t105`; the lap is about 1.38 × the reference. ## What the controller does - **Estimator:** it integrates the gyro and accelerometer and corrects position, velocity and tilt from each delayed tracker frame. Errors against ground truth are about 1 cm and 0.02 rad. - **Reference:** it hovers on the start point for 2 s (off the clock), then follows a precomputed smooth curve through the four gate centres. The curve was optimised offline with scipy for minimum time under thrust, rotation-rate and minimum-thrust limits, and is embedded as numbers in the file. - **Control:** a position loop feeds a thrust-direction and body-rate cascade. The collective thrust is corrected from the accelerometer, which absorbs the unknown mass, thrust coefficient and drag. - **Timing:** about 0.13 ms per call. ## Findings that made the difference - **Position loop on the centre of mass.** The tracked point hangs 5.4 cm below the centre of mass.
