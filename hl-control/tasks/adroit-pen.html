Adroit ShadowHand (MuJoCo, Gymnasium-Robotics) · solved 6 of 6 agent attempts pass

The task. Dexterous manipulation with no task-space interface: the controller maps the task's own observation vector to one position-servo set point per joint (24 hand joints plus the arm's) every 10 ms, 200 steps per episode, contact-rich, on the Adroit suite of gymnasium-robotics 1.4.2.

more

The field's method is reinforcement learning with demonstrations (DAPG); its released policy is measured in rl_references/adroit. Built as the frontier tier of the family and open for scripted control when it was built: in-hand reorientation of a free pen with 24 wrist and finger servos and no arm, from lying across the palm to an axis near the vertical. The gate is 0.9 of the incumbent's own rate: the DAPG policy passes hold on 82.5 % of the grader's seeds (and on 79.5 % of 200 public seeds), so the usual 0.9 would ask for more than the field's method delivers; the gate is 0.74. Zero action passes hold on 1/120 fresh seeds, and a fixed hand posture with the wrist tilted toward the target, searched with the repository tuner (the solution until 2026-11-23), on 4/120. Solved since then without the incumbent, by offline search rather than by feedback: the nominal plant is deterministic and every episode starts from the same hand and pen state, so only the target changes, and a table of 508 open-loop posture scripts found with CMA-ES on the simulator, one picked per episode from the target axis, holds on 120/120 fresh seeds. The gate therefore does not require feedback; under changed mass, friction or servo gain the same table holds on 0.39-0.55 (the search's measurement).

Gate. per-seed success is the `hold` rung of this benchmark's ladder ['contact', 'partial', 'success', 'hold'] (`--success-mode hold`), not the evaluator's default flag; the task is solved when the success rate over the grader's held-out seeds is `>= 0.74`
