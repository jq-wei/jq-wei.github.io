adroit-pen-robust

Adroit ShadowHand (MuJoCo, Gymnasium-Robotics) · solved 5 of 6 agent attempts pass

The task. Dexterous manipulation with no task-space interface: the controller maps the task's own observation vector to one position-servo set point per joint (24 hand joints plus the arm's) every 10 ms, 200 steps per episode, contact-rich, on the Adroit suite of gymnasium-robotics 1.4.2.

more

The field's method is reinforcement learning with demonstrations (DAPG); its released policy is measured in rl_references/adroit. The pen gate on the nominal plant and on six sibling plants at once: pen 50 % heavier, friction 30 % lower, servos 20 % softer, observation noise of 0.005, target range 25 % wider, and a displaced start of the pen (up to 0.015 m in x and in y and up to 0.3 rad about the vertical, drawn per seed). One rate gates all seven plants: 0.45, which is 0.9 of the lowest of the incumbent's seven hold rates on the grader's seeds (50.0 % on shaken_v1; all seven: nominal_v0 67.5 %, heavy_v1 70.0 %, slippery_v1 67.5 %, weak_v1 70.0 %, noisy_v1 72.5 %, wide_v1 80.0 %, shaken_v1 50.0 %). On 200 public seeds the policy holds on 79.5 % (nominal), 72.5 % (heavy), 73.5 % (friction), 58.0 % (servos), 80.0 % (noise), 69.5 % (wide), 46.0 % (displaced). Built so that the open-loop answer to adroit-pen does not carry over: that task's reference, a table of posture scripts picked from the target axis, holds on 1.00 of 100 seeds on the nominal, noise and wide plants and on 0.55 (heavy), 0.39 (friction), 0.49 (servos) and 0.26 (displaced), because it relies on every episode starting from the same state and on the same set points moving the pen the same way. The reference here closes the loop through a copy of the plant: it reads the start state from the observation, finds out which of the listed physics it is on from the first 20 steps, and plays candidate scripts on the matching copy before it commits to one and again when the pen is not where the plan put it; it holds on 120/120 (nominal), 94/120 (heavy), 84/120 (friction), 81/120 (servos), 106/120 (noise), 120/120 (wide), 88/120 (displaced) fresh seeds. Its misses on the mass, friction and gain siblings are plans made at step 0 on the nominal copy that the correction at step 20 cannot save, and pens dropped during a script.

Gate. per-seed success is the `hold` rung of this benchmark's ladder ['contact', 'partial', 'success', 'hold'] (`--success-mode hold`), not the evaluator's default flag; the task is solved when the success rate over the grader's held-out seeds is `>= 0.45`; the same gate must also hold on the sibling scenario(s) `heavy_v1`, `slippery_v1`, `weak_v1`, `noisy_v1`, `wide_v1`, `shaken_v1` (`--scenarios`)
