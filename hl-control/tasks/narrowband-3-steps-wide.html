Every modal frequency is drawn within +-4 % of the nominal model instead of +-2 % (dampings +-10 % as before), so the lightly damped modes next to the band (45 and 109 Hz) and the mode inside it (72 Hz) sit up to 1.8, 4.4 and 2.9 Hz from where the model has them; disturbance law and measurements are those of narrowband-3-steps. The gate is the rung landau_ma: in every segment, on 0.9 of the seeds, global attenuation of 30 dB, 40 dB at every tone and amplification within the published level-3 limit elsewhere. The published transient limit (landau_tr), which is the gate of narrowband-3-steps, is the top rung of the ladder here and is open: the best known controller reaches it on 32/40 held-out seeds and on 192/240 fresh seeds (0.80). The gate was first set on that rung, with no passing controller; it was moved one rung down the declared ladder on 2026-11-23 so that the task has a passing reference, and no threshold of any rung was changed.

Gate. per-seed success is the `landau_ma` rung of this benchmark's ladder ['stable', 'attenuate', 'landau_ga', 'landau_da', 'landau_ma', 'landau_tr'] (`--success-mode landau_ma`), not the evaluator's default flag; the task is solved when the success rate over the grader's held-out seeds is `>= 0.9`
